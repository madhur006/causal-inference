# Difference in Difference 
estimates the causal effect of a policy, treatment, or intervention by comparing the pre- and post-intervention changes in outcomes between a treatment group and a control group.

# Interrupted time series  
evaluate the causal impact of an intervention, policy, or program implemented at a specific, known point in time.
by tracking a population outcome continuously over multiple time points before and after the event, ITS controls for underlying secular trends—allowing us to determine whether the intervention produced a statistically significant change beyond what would have occurred naturally.
